# 🎮 Single-File HTML Apps & Games

A curated collection of zero-dependency, single-file browser games and utility web apps designed for instant fun, focus, and relaxation. Every project lives inside a standalone `.html` file—click any **Play In Browser** or **Open In Browser** button below to render the raw HTML file live in your browser (tip: `Ctrl+Click` or middle-click to open in a new tab).

---

## 🕹️ Browser Games

### 1. Tactical Breach — 3D First-Person Wave Shooter
[![Play In Browser](https://shields.io▶_Play_In_Browser-EF4444?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./Tactical%20Breach.html)

Hold the arena through escalating **Heat Waves** and spend earned kill points in the **Requisition** shop between breaches.
* **3 Tactical Maps:** *Container Yard* (stacked containers & stepped platforms), *Industrial Plant* (dim warehouse with steel columns & mezzanine), and *Rooftops* (open-air gaps & ramps).
* **3 Difficulty Tiers:** *Recruit* (health regen), *Veteran* (standard threat + regen), and *Spec Ops* (tougher, accurate enemies + medkits only).
* **Requisition Armory:** Unlock the **MP5 SMG** (`1500 pts`), **Pump Shotgun** (`2000 pts`), **Ammo Refills** (`300 pts`), and **Field Medkits** (`800 pts`).
* **Controls:** `WASD` Move · `Shift` Sprint · `Space` Jump · `Mouse` Look · `Left Click` Fire · `Right Click` Aim Down Sights · `R` Reload · `1 2 3` Switch Weapon.

### 2. Neon Swarm — Reverse Bullet-Hell Arena Roguelite
[![Play In Browser](https://shields.io▶_Play_In_Browser-8B5CF6?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./neon-swarm.html)

Fast-paced top-down survival roguelite where auto-firing weapons let you focus purely on dodging swarms and chaining upgrades.

### 3. Fruit Jar — Physics Fruit Merge Puzzle
[![Play In Browser](https://shields.io▶_Play_In_Browser-F59E0B?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./FRUIT%20JAR.html)

## 🛠️ Utility & Lifestyle Web Apps

### 5. Command Center — Zero-Setup Live Global Dashboard
[![Open In Browser](https://shields.io🚀_Open_In_Browser-10B981?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./Command%20Center.html)

A real-time intelligence and daily briefing dashboard powered by keyless, CORS-safe public HTTPS APIs.
* **7 Live Modules:** **Weather & Air** city lookup, **Crypto · 24h** market tracker, **Currency Converter**, **Tech Pulse** (Hacker News top stories), **Seismic Monitor** (M4.5+ global earthquakes), **Daily Wisdom** quotes, and **Word of the Day**.
* **Controls:** Includes one-click **Refresh All Data** and individual widget retry/copy actions.

### 6. Night In — All-in-One Evening Planner
[![Open In Browser](https://shields.io🚀_Open_In_Browser-EC4899?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./NIGHT%20IN.html)

Plan your entire evening in one place—combining food recipes, TV discovery, and background music.
* **What to Eat:** Search recipes by ingredient or roll the **Surprise Dinner** generator.
* **What to Watch:** Browse TV shows or use **Quick-Decide Swipe** mode to pick tonight's watch.
* **What to Hear:** Search and play music previews on a persistent bottom audio player while browsing other tabs.
* **Tonight's Lineup:** Pin your chosen meal, show, and tracks into a shareable evening summary drawer.

### 7. Stillwater — Procedural Ambient Soundscape Mixer
[![Open In Browser](https://shields.io🚀_Open_In_Browser-3B82F6?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./Stillwater.html)

A calming Web Audio API sound generator that synthesizes six layered soundscapes mathematically with zero audio loops.
* **Features:** One-click mood presets (**Deep Work**, **Rainy Night**, **Cozy Cabin**), individual channel sliders including a **40Hz Binaural** focus channel (best with headphones), **Master Volume**, and a built-in **Sleep/Focus Timer** (`15m`, `30m`, `60m`, `Off`).

### 8. Infinite Board — Spatial Mind-Map & Sticky Canvas
[![Open In Browser](https://shields.io🚀_Open_In_Browser-6366F1?style=for-the-badge)](https://githack.com) [![View Code](https://shields.ioView_Code-1F2937?style=for-the-badge)](./infinite-board.html)

An offline-first visual brainstorming canvas for sticky notes, node linking, and diagramming.

## 💻 How to Run Offline on Your Computer

1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** (or clone via `git clone https://github.com`).
2. Double-click any `.html` file to open it directly in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
